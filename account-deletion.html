Delete your TrackAllerg account and data

Fastest way: in the app

Open TrackAllerg → Settings → Data → Delete account. You will re-authenticate with Google, then all your diary data and your account are permanently deleted from our servers. This is immediate and irreversible.

Without access to the app

Email hi@trackallerg.net from the Google account email you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my TrackAllerg account". Deletion is completed within 30 days and covers: all diary entries (symptoms, medicines, notes, locations, environment snapshots) and the account record itself.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: everything stored server-side — there is no server-side data other than your diary and account record.
  • Kept: nothing. Anonymous crash reports (if you had crash reporting enabled) contain no account identifier and cannot be linked to you; they expire per Firebase Crashlytics retention (90 days).
  • Data on your own device (guest-mode data or cached copies) is under your control: uninstalling the app or Settings → Delete removes it.
